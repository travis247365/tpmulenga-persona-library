Setting up in claude.ai — once

  1. Go to claude.ai/design.
  2. Open the "Design system" dropdown in the prompt bar. It reads None by default.
  3. Choose "TPCL — Travis Paul Consulting". Claude now reads the brand rules before it draws anything.
  4. Leave "Template" on None. That picker holds Anthropic's own starter layouts. The TPCL templates live inside the design system — you point at them by name in your prompt ("use the WhatsApp Status template").

Everything is self-contained. The Poppins typeface and the Travis Paul signature are embedded in the system, so your output is on-brand whether or not the font is installed on your machine — or on the machine of whoever you send it to.

03 · Judging the output

Claude will get it close. You decide if it is right.

The full rules are in TPCL_Brand_Guidelines_v1.0.pdf. These are the ones that decide most reviews:

The nine pre-flight checks

  1. Two to three motifs — not one, not five.
  2. Accent block top-left; signature diagonally opposite.
  3. CAPS on charcoal, sentence case on white.
  4. No divider line where dark and light meet.
  5. Charcoal labels on every cyan or amber fill.
  1. Square corners, no shadows, no gradients.
  2. Logo clear space at 45% of its height, unbroken.
  3. Every figure tagged Measured / Modelled / Assumed.
  4. Every total reconciles to source.

The last two are not design rules. They are the reason the design exists — TPCL sells evidence, and a modelled number presented as measured is the one reputational mistake that cannot be walked back. If a provenance tag crowds the layout, change the layout.

Four things the system will not do for you

  • Email cannot carry the embedded font or the embedded logo. Gmail strips webfonts; Gmail and Outlook both drop embedded images. Host the logo at an https URL and accept the Century Gothic fallback. Do not work around it with pictures of text.
  • Safe-zone bands are drawn guides, not enforcement. They are marked "no-export" and must be deleted before you export. Nothing stops you exporting with them on.
  • The logo is raster at 760px wide. Fine to about 700px on screen. For large print, go back to the vector masters in the TPCL logo library.
  • Poppins is bundled as a Latin subset. Another script or language needs the font re-cut.

If you need something the templates do not cover

Describe the surface and its exact pixel dimensions, and say which existing template is closest. Claude will extend the system rather than invent a new one — for example: "Build a 1080×1080 event announcement. Closest template is the social square, but I need a date block and a venue line under the headline."

One thing to keep an eye on. The logo carries its own colours — deep blue and a light cyan — which are close to, but not the same as, the system's cyan accent. Never put the full-colour logo on a surface that also has a cyan accent block; use the black or white version there. Page 4 of the guidelines explains why.
