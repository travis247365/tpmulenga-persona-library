Rules

  • Three to five items. Six or more is a table, not a timeline.
  • Every item carries a time label in cyan Medium uppercase — weeks, quarters or days. A timeline without dates is just a list.
  • Dot halo matches the background, so the dot punches through the rule. On white it is a white halo; on charcoal, charcoal.
  • Never combine the timeline with outline rectangles on the same page — both are cyan stroke work.
  • Steps are square; the timeline dot is the only circle in the system.
