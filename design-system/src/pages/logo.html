Logo & lockups

The mark is the handwritten Travis Paul script with CONSULTING in tracked caps beneath it. Aspect ratio 1.93:1. Three cuts — white, black, full colour. Consulting is the default on every client-facing artefact; Holdings only appears on parent-entity material.

The three cuts

White — on charcoal
Black — on white
Full colour

Full colour is for light backgrounds with no competing colour — covers, letterheads, the website footer. On any slide that already carries cyan or amber, use black or white so the accent stays the only colour event.

A fourth approved treatment — tint

The 2021 brand master approves four treatments, not three: full colour, black, tint, and reversed white. The tint sets the script at #57585A with the tracked caps at a lighter step — for watermarks, document backgrounds, and partner walls where the mark must be present but must not compete. Never use tint below 40% or the caps disappear.

These are two different eras and they do not match. The 2021 logo master is built on deep blue #085F89, light cyan #59D1E2 and pure black #000000. The layout system derived from the business-profile deck uses charcoal #3A3A3A and cyan #00BCD4.

The ruling: the logo is a fixed asset and keeps its own colours — do not recolour it to match the palette. The layout keeps charcoal and system cyan. But because #59D1E2 and #00BCD4 are close without being equal, never place the full-colour logo on the same surface as a cyan accent block — the near-miss reads as a mistake. Use the black or white cut there instead. That is why the deck templates use black-on-white and white-on-charcoal everywhere.

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space = 45% of the logo height on all four sides (equivalently 23.5% of its width — the padding is uniform, not proportional to the side). Measured off the 2021 brand master travis paul_logo_Clear Space.ai: logo bbox 232 × 121, padding 54–55px on every side. Nothing enters it — no text, no rule, no accent block, no photo edge.

This is roughly double the clear space most people leave by eye. At a 34px slide-footer logo that is 15px of protected space all round; at 120px wide (62px tall) it is 28px.

Minimum sizes. Screen / social: 120px wide. Slide footer: 34px tall. Print: 28mm wide. Below that the script closes up and the caps disappear — use the initials-free charcoal wordmark instead, or drop the logo entirely.

Don't

✕ Place on cyan or amber. The accent is not a background.
✕ Stretch or condense. Ratio is locked at 1.93:1.
✕ Use black on a mid-tone. Below ~4.5:1, switch to white.
✕ Straddle two tones or sit on a busy photo. Add a solid pad.
