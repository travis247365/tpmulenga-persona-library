Colour

Six tokens, two modes. Charcoal and white do all the work; cyan, amber and blue-gray are accents with strict jobs. Nothing outside this list is a TPCL colour.

Rules that follow from those numbers

  1. Cyan buttons carry charcoal text, never white. White on cyan is 2.30:1 and fails.
  2. On white, cyan and amber are graphic-only — accent blocks, rules, dots, borders, fills behind dark text. Never body copy, never a link colour on white.
  3. On charcoal, cyan and amber are safe as text (4.95:1 and 6.98:1). This is why the cyan timeline and cyan numerals live on dark slides.
  4. Blue-gray is a surface. It is 1.75:1 on white — it can never be read as text on white.
  5. One accent per surface. Cyan and amber do not appear on the same slide or post except where amber is the accent block and cyan is a data mark.

Client-overlay slot

Engagement work tints exactly one variable — --tp-client — and swaps the co-brand logo. The charcoal/white frame, Poppins and the motifs stay TPCL. Precedent: Zamtel green #28B04C, Discover blue #3865B0. Check the client colour against charcoal and white before using it as text.
