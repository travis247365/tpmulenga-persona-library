The dashed bands are guides, not artwork — delete them before export. Instagram overlays your profile row and progress bar in the top ~250px and the reply bar in the bottom ~250px, so all type and the signature live inside the middle 1420px. Background colour still fills the full 1920 — only content respects the safe area. Headline 94px caps, three to six words. Step boxes carry the sequence; if the story is a single statement, drop them and let the headline take the whole frame. A Reel cover uses this same file with the numbered list removed, because Instagram crops the cover to 1:1 in the profile grid — keep anything that must survive that crop inside the centre square.
