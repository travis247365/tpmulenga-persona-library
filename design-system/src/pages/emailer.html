Email is the one place the design system bends. Gmail strips @font-face, so Poppins only renders for recipients who already have it installed — the stack falls back to Century Gothic, then Helvetica/Arial. That is expected; do not chase it with images of text.

What is load-bearing here: tables with role="presentation", not flexbox or grid, because Outlook on Windows renders through Word; every style inline; 600px fixed width; a single column; no background images (Outlook drops them, and the charcoal masthead must survive); the CTA is a padded <a> inside a coloured <td> so the whole block is tappable at 44px+; and the cyan button carries a charcoal label, because white on cyan is 2.30:1.
