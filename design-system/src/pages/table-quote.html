Rules

  • No vertical rules, no zebra stripes, no cell fills. One 2px charcoal rule under the header, 1px hairlines between rows.
  • Numbers right-aligned and tabular; text left-aligned. Never centre a column.
  • Header labels are 12px Medium uppercase +.1em muted — quieter than the data, because the data is the point.
  • Totals row is bold, no fill, and it must reconcile. A total that does not tie to source does not ship.
  • A source line sits under every table. If the data is modelled, the line says so.
  • Quotes take a 3px cyan left rule and no quotation marks. Attribution below, name in Medium.
