1080 × 1920 (9:16) · WhatsApp Status

WhatsApp eats more of the frame than Instagram does — the progress bar, contact name and timestamp sit in the top ~260px, and the caption strip plus the “Reply” swipe-up handle occupy the bottom ~320px. Content lives in the middle 1340px. The dashed bands are guides; delete them before export.

Status is read in under three seconds, usually one-handed on a mid-range Android in daylight — so this template is light mode with one very large figure. Do not put a URL on a Status: it is not tappable. Use the amber CTA to point somewhere the viewer already is (“Full note in bio”, “Reply for the pack”), and rely on the reply thread. Export as JPEG under 1 MB; WhatsApp recompresses hard, and flat charcoal-on-white with no gradients survives that compression cleanly.
