  You asked me to reference the brand guidelines PDF — there wasn't one

  No TPCL Brand Guidelines.pdf existed. What did exist were two 2021 Illustrator
  masters in your logo zip: travis paul_Proper use of Colour.ai and travis 
  paul_logo_Clear Space.ai. They're PDF-compatible, so I rendered and read them
  rather than writing guidelines from my own inference.

  That turned up three corrections to what I gave you earlier — one of them 
  mine:

  1. Clear space is 45% of the logo height, not ~24%. Measured off the master:
  bbox 232×121, padding 54–55px uniform. My earlier "2× the cap-height of
  CONSULTING" was inferred, and it was roughly half the real requirement.
  Corrected in the system and pushed to Claude Design.
  2. Four approved treatments, not three. I'd documented
  white/black/full-colour. The master also approves a tint at #57585A for
  watermarks and document grounds.
  3. The logo's colours are not the layout palette. The 2021 master is deep blue
  #085F89, caps cyan #59D1E2, pure black #000000. The deck-derived system is
  charcoal #3A3A3A + cyan #00BCD4. Ruling in the guidelines: never recolour the
  logo — but never put the full-colour logo on a surface carrying a cyan accent 
  block, because #59D1E2 against #00BCD4 is a near-miss that reads as a
  production error. Page 4 of the guidelines is devoted to this.
