# Persona library

Five AI personas, each encoding one domain Travis Paul Mulenga has actually
worked in. They serve two purposes at once: they are usable system prompts, and
they are a portfolio — each one is evidence of a skillset, with the engagements
that back it named underneath.

**Every claim in every persona traces to the CV or a signed document.** Nothing
here is aspirational. Where a persona says it knows something, the Evidence
section says where that came from. That constraint is the point: a portfolio that
overstates is worth less than one that does not, because the first thing a
reader does is test it.

## Rules that apply to all five

- Never name a d2c Telcare client. Their contracts forbid it (Sanlam cl. 8.3.3,
  RDG cl. 8.2, Sun King MSA cl. 7.1–7.2). Sector descriptions only.
- Never quote a ZMW figure; none reconciles to a source record.
- The Henley award is a **Postgraduate Diploma in Management Practice**, not an
  MBA. There is no law qualification.
- Say "led delivery of" rather than "built" where a vendor product was involved.
