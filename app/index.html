Persona Forge · bring your own key

Your CV, read properly

Paste a CV and get five working personas — each a usable system prompt, each backed by the engagement it came from. Then score any job advert against them.

Where your data goes

Nowhere but Anthropic. This page has no backend and no database. Your CV and your API key stay in this browser and are sent only to api.anthropic.com, directly from your machine. Nobody — including whoever published this page — can read either. That is why it needs your own key rather than offering a free tier.

04Turn on the job feed

From here on it runs without you. You get one forwarding address and add one filter in whatever mail client you already use; every job alert that arrives is parsed, de-duplicated and stacked below. No Google sign-in, no permissions to grant, nothing to revoke later except the filter itself.

Forward your job alerts to this address:

In Gmail: Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter. Put jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com in From, choose Forward it to, and pick the address above. Repeat for any other job board you subscribe to.

Keep the address. It is the whole credential — there is no password and no recovery. Anyone who has it can post into your feed, and losing it means starting a new one.

Personas are generated from what the CV actually says. The prompt instructs the model not to invent credentials, employers or figures, and to leave a claim out rather than guess — a portfolio gets tested before it gets believed. Verify every generated line against your own documents before using it anywhere.
