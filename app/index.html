Where your data goes

Nowhere but Anthropic. This page has no backend and no database. Your CV and your API key stay in this browser and are sent only to api.anthropic.com, directly from your machine. Nobody — including whoever published this page — can read either. That is why it needs your own key rather than offering a free tier.
